# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in the Vladmir project respectful and constructive for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and technical work in good faith.
- Accept constructive criticism and focus on what is best for the project.
- Protect other people's privacy. Do not publish credentials, personal data, or private conversations.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate exposure of private information, and disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for conduct that violates this policy. Report concerns privately through the repository's security/contact channel. This project is maintained on a best-effort basis and does not promise an enforcement SLA.
